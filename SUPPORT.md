## Where to ask

- **Questions and how-to help:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Shuvam-Banerji-Seal/gmail-drive-storage-lens/discussions). Search existing topics first.
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Shuvam-Banerji-Seal/gmail-drive-storage-lens/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the steps you took, what you expected, what happened, your browser and operating system, and any error text. Do not include your Client ID, tokens, email addresses, or message contents. Redact them before posting.
- **Feature ideas:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/Shuvam-Banerji-Seal/gmail-drive-storage-lens/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml), or start a Discussion if you want to talk it through first.
- **Security problems:** do not use public channels. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the reporting section of the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Before you open a bug report

1. Make sure you are on the latest version (the live site is always built from `main`).
2. Check whether the issue is about your Google account setup, which is outside the app. Errors such as `origin_mismatch`, `access_denied`, `accessNotConfigured`, and `admin_policy_enforced` are explained in the troubleshooting table.
3. Try a private or new browser window with extensions disabled.

## What we cannot help with

- Changing settings in your Google account or Google Cloud project. Google's own help pages and support channels are the right place for those.
- Recovering files or messages you deleted. Check Gmail's and Drive's Trash first; items there are kept for a limited time.
- Problems in forks or copies run by other people.

## Response expectations

This is a volunteer-run open-source project. We read every issue and discussion, but we cannot guarantee a response time. Clear, reproducible reports get the fastest fixes.
