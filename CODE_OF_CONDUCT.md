# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Gmail Storage Lens community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the community as a whole.
- Writing clear bug reports, and treating people who are new to the project with patience.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Attempts to collect other people's personal data, mailbox contents, or account information, including in issues and pull requests.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and they may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, the wiki, and any event or chat space associated with the project. It also applies when an individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour:

- For sensitive concerns, use GitHub's private reporting: open the **Security** tab of the repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. This creates a private advisory visible only to maintainers. Describe the conduct concern there and say that it is a Code of Conduct report, not a security issue.
- For less sensitive matters, you may open a [Discussion](https://github.com/Shuvam-Banerji-Seal/gmail-drive-storage-lens/discussions) asking a maintainer to contact you, but do not post personal details publicly.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity confidential where possible, and respond as soon as they reasonably can. Everyone involved is expected to cooperate with the review.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on a consequence. They may skip steps for severe violations.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behaviour that is not welcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private written note from maintainers explaining the issue and why it matters, with a request to correct the behaviour. Public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code of Conduct, for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces and external channels.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained harassment, or an attack on a class of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
