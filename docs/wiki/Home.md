# Gmail Storage Lens wiki

Welcome. Gmail Storage Lens is an open-source, browser-only tool that shows what is using your Gmail and Google Drive storage, lets you explore it, and helps you clean it up safely.

It runs in your browser with no server of its own. You create your own Google OAuth client, so your access stays under your control.

**Live app:** https://shuvam-banerji-seal.github.io/gmail-drive-storage-lens/
