# Getting started

This page is a quick tour. For the exact steps to connect your account, follow [[OAuth Setup|OAuth-Setup]].

## Try it first

You can explore every view without a Google account. On the landing page, choose **Explore the demo**. The demo uses clearly labelled synthetic data, and nothing is sent to Google.

## What you need

- A Google account (personal, or a Workspace account that allows third-party apps).
- A web browser that is up to date (a recent version of Firefox, Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
- About five minutes to create a free Google Cloud project and OAuth client.

You do not need to pay for anything, and you do not need to enter billing details.

## The three steps

1. **Create your own OAuth client.** This is a one-time setup in Google's console. It gives the app permission to sign you in, and it belongs only to you.
2. **Paste the Client ID** into the setup wizard. The Client ID is public, so it is safe to paste. The app never asks for a client secret. The wizard checks the format and runs a connection test.
3. **Sign in and explore.** The app starts with read-only access. You choose what to clean, and it asks for broader permission only at that point.

## What to expect the first time

- Google shows a warning that the app has not been verified. This is normal for your own app. Continue past it.
- Only accounts you added as test users can sign in.
- The first scan can take a few minutes for a large mailbox. The largest messages are scanned first, and you can explore results while the scan continues.
- The app shows a short guided tour on first use. You can restart it from Settings.
